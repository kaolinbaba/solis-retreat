What is Solis Retreat?+

Solis is a fictional wellness retreat created as an interactive design demo. The landscape, experiences, and stay planner illustrate a hospitality concept.

Do I have to follow a schedule?+

The concept is built around choice. The day examples are invitations, with room to spend time quietly or explore at your own pace.

Can I make a reservation here?+

You can try the stay planner and download your sample plan. This demo does not check availability, send an inquiry, or take payment.
